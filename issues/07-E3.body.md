## User story

As the bid team, we want a morning digest in Teams and email, so that we see new opportunities before the day starts.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The digest posts daily to the Teams channel and by email
- [x] Only open, actionable notices are included
- [x] Closed and awarded notices are excluded

## Details

- **Epic:** E3 Alerts and digest
- **Priority:** Must
- **Requirements:** F4, N5
- **Status:** Delivered
